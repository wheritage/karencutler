Hi, I'm Karen. I coach people to get stronger in a way that feels good, physically and mentally.

Always learning: keeping my coaching current at fitness and performance conferences.

Why I coach

I became a trainer because I believe fitness should make your life better, not become another source of stress. My job isn't to push you until it hurts. It's to help you build strength, confidence and habits that actually last.

Every body is different, so every plan is too. We work on movement that feels right for you, at a pace that keeps you motivated, and we celebrate progress along the way.

I work with people at every level, and I have a particular focus on women going through perimenopause and menopause, a stage where the right training makes a real difference.

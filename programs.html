Train your way.

Four ways to work with me. Pick one to start, or combine them. We'll adjust as you go.

1-on-1 personal training

Best if you want hands-on coaching and accountability.

Private, in-person sessions built entirely around you. Every workout is adjusted in real time, so you're challenged without being overwhelmed.

  • Personalized programming
  • Form and technique coaching on every lift
  • Progress tracked session to session

Custom online program

Best if your schedule changes or you train on your own.

A plan written for you to follow at home, at your gym or on the road, with regular check-ins so you're never guessing.

  • Your plan, on your phone
  • Video demos for every exercise
  • Check-ins and adjustments

Strength and mobility

Best if you feel stiff, sit a lot or want to protect your joints.

Functional strength and restored range of motion, so everyday movement feels easier and your body holds up for the long run.

  • Joint-friendly strength foundations
  • Mobility and flexibility work
  • Posture and injury prevention

Nutrition and habits

Best alongside any program, to support your results.

Practical guidance to fuel training and recovery. No crash diets, no guilt, just habits that fit the way you actually live.

  • Everyday nutrition basics
  • Habits that stick
  • Sleep, stress and recovery

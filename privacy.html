This policy explains how Karen Cutler collects, uses and protects your personal information, in line with Québec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25).

Who is responsible

Karen Cutler is responsible for protecting your personal information. For any question or request, write to cutlerkarenann@gmail.com or call 514-606-1256.

What we collect

When you send a request through this website: your name, email, phone number if you provide it, your training goals and preferences, and any health information you choose to share, such as injuries or life stage.

Why we collect it

Only to reply to your request, plan your training safely and keep in touch about your sessions. Your information is never sold or shared for marketing.

Where it is stored

Requests are delivered to Karen's email through the FormSubmit service and may be kept in a private Google Sheet. These providers may store data outside Québec.

How long we keep it

Requests that don't lead to training are deleted after 12 months. Client information is kept while you train and deleted on request after that.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, at any time by contacting Karen.

Health disclaimer

The information on this website is general and does not replace medical advice. Talk to your doctor before starting a new exercise program, especially if you have a health condition, an injury or are going through menopause-related treatment.

Menopause is not the finish line.

Your body changes through perimenopause and menopause. With training built for those changes, this can be one of the strongest chapters of your life.

What changes, and how we train for it

Pick a topic to see how training adapts. Every plan is still built for you personally.

What can happen

Lower estrogen is linked to faster bone loss, which raises the risk of fractures later on.

How we train

Progressive strength work and safe impact, which are among the best-studied ways to help maintain bone density.

What can happen

Muscle mass tends to decline with age, affecting strength, balance and metabolism.

How we train

Lifting with good technique and steady progression to build and keep muscle you'll use every day.

What can happen

Hot flashes, disrupted sleep and fatigue can make it hard to stay consistent.

How we train

Sessions dosed to your energy that day, so training lifts you up instead of draining you.

What can happen

Mood shifts and a changing body can shake your confidence.

How we train

Clear goals and visible progress, in a supportive space where you feel capable again.

What we focus on

Four priorities guide every session for women in this stage of life.

Bone and joint health

Strength work that supports bone density and keeps joints resilient.

Muscle and metabolism

Build and preserve muscle to support strength and everyday energy.

Recovery and sleep

Training volume that respects recovery, so you rest better, not worse.

Confidence

Movement that helps you feel like yourself again, capable and at ease.

Training complements, but doesn't replace, medical care. For symptoms or treatment questions, talk to your doctor.
